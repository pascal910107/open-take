## Cases

[`cases/README.md`](../cases/README.md) (`references/cases/` in the skill;
`skill cases` prints it, `skill cases <name>` one case, and `skill film`
prints the folder's path) takes launch films apart segment by segment: what
moves, what holds, how each part hands over, what the sound does, the
material — including one human-made launch film and one film made without
open-take, which rebuilt the product's components as its own objects. Its
films are built in different ways, side by side. It is indexed by what a
segment wants to say ("show a result appearing from an input", "let it
breathe"). Each case is another product's material — not a template or a
rule.
