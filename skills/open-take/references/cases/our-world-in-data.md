# Our World in Data — 15 seconds, vertical, number first

A 15 s, 1080×1920 (vertical) film of ourworldindata.org, made in October 2026
by Opus 5.5 writing its own film page. The first frame is already a huge
number; one fact, its proof, one interaction, breadth, brand.

**Brief it was given:** a 15-second vertical (9:16) social short that
grabs people in the first 2 seconds. Everything else was the model's choice.

## Structure: fact → proof → touch → breadth → brand

Everything sits on the site's deep blue, so changes read as one surface
(measured: no change above 0.30; above 0.12 only every half second from 9.6
to 11.6 s, the dealt cards).

## Material

- **Stills of the real site** at 3× (the chart component at phone width):
  the line chart, the map at six years after a real click on Map, a real
  hover tooltip, five other charts.
- **The product's public data** for the opening count (looked up per year,
  not interpolated — an interpolated first version showed "72 years in
  2021", which the data does not say).
- The site's fonts and colours.

## Techniques worth seeing

- **No black first frame.** The fact is on screen at t = 0.
- **A chart that draws itself** by uncovering a still of the real chart with
  a moving mask.
- **A count driven by the real series**, so every frame shows a true pair.
- **Layout for a phone**: the chart taken at the site's narrow breakpoint,
  pushes chosen so the tooltip text is readable at phone size.
