## Checks

Each round, before anything new: every change the last round asked for, looked for on the new draft at its second — a still or the sheet, not the notes. A change written down as made is not a change seen.

Before the master, each one marked yes, no or not looked at:

- every boundary in the render's strip: something crosses, or the cut is meant
- each transition at 0.1 s a frame: every frame has one thing to look at
- after each camera move: the words inside the frame's safe margin, readable at phone size (`film probe`, text lane)
- the held frames of each stretch, small in a row with the words covered: the sections and the light and dark between them can be told apart
- every stretch called still: something of the product happens in it, or it is gone
- the timing measured (`film listen`, `film probe`), and the whole film met as a viewer meets it, not only the seconds that changed (`film viewer` on this cut, or the sheet and frames in order): two different things, each marked
