Recorded instruments in this folder: where each comes from, its licence, and
whether a film that uses it must credit it. manifest.json says the same per
instrument ("license", "credit"); lib/sampler.mjs credits() lists the ones a
score used, for tl.credit on the page. All were trimmed, resampled and
re-encoded (opus/flac) from the sources below.

MUST BE CREDITED (CC BY 3.0)

  piano       grand piano, A0–C8, 4 velocity layers
              Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm — CC BY 3.0
              Credit it in the film's credits, naming the licence and that it
              was changed, e.g.:
              "Piano: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0), trimmed, resampled and re-encoded"
              On the page:
              tl.credit({ what: "piano samples", by: "Salamander Grand Piano V3, Alexander Holm",
                licence: "CC BY 3.0", text: "trimmed, resampled and re-encoded" })
